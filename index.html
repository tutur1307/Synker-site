Synker

Synker keeps your game saves in sync between modded consoles, Android handhelds and PC emulators.

You run a small hub at home, on a NAS or a PC. Each device syncs with it, so you can play on one device and continue on another. The hub keeps a history of every version, and asks you before overwriting anything.

Google Drive

The hub can keep a copy of itself in a folder of your Google Drive, so that your saves survive a lost disk or PC. This is optional. You connect your own Google account from the hub's settings, and Synker only gets access to the files it creates, not to the rest of your Drive.

See the privacy policy for what this means for your data.

Status

Synker is a personal project, in early development, and is not publicly released yet.

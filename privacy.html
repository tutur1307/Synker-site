Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Synker is software you run yourself. Your saves stay on your own hub and your own devices. There is no Synker server or Synker account, and Synker's developer receives none of your data.

What Synker accesses in Google Drive

Connecting Google Drive is optional. If you do, Synker asks Google for the drive.file permission only, which covers the files and folders Synker creates itself. It cannot see or change anything else in your Drive.

In the folder you choose, Synker writes a copy of your hub: its database and your save archives. It reads them back when you restore a hub, and deletes the archives the hub no longer keeps.

What your hub keeps

To keep the copy going, your hub stores two things in its own database: the access token Google gives it, and the email address of the connected Google account, which it shows in its settings.

Who receives your data

Your hub talks directly to Google. Your Google data does not go through anyone else, and it is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics. Google's handling of the files in your Drive is covered by Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Removing access and data

Disconnect in the hub's settings withdraws Synker's access. You can also withdraw it from your Google account's permissions. The copy stays in your Drive until you delete its folder there, and everything on your hub stays under your control.

Contact

For any question, open an issue on this site's repository. Issues are public, so please do not include personal data.
